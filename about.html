Leadership

FOUNDER & MANAGING PARTNER

Mr. E. Sekar

With the world-class technical skills and management expertise gained by serving for more than fifteen years abroad with companies of international repute such as M/s. National Petroleum Construction Company (Abu Dhabi), the founder Mr. E. Sekar commenced the business in 1995.

The enthusiastic and visionary founder Mr. Sekar has been leading the company for over 30 years and has brought gradual, steady growth that has established Sekar Constructions as a prominent Class-I civil construction company, identified for its excellent quality, ethical standards, sound systems, and strong credibility.
