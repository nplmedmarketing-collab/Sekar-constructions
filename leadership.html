Founder & Partners

Leadership

FOUNDER & MANAGING PARTNER

Mr. E. Sekar

With the world-class technical skills and management expertise gained by serving for more than fifteen years abroad with companies of international repute such as M/s. National Petroleum Construction Company (Abu Dhabi), the founder Mr. E. Sekar commenced the business in 1995.

The enthusiastic and visionary founder Mr. Sekar has been leading the company for over 30 years and has brought gradual, steady growth that has established Sekar Constructions as a prominent Class-I civil construction company, identified for its excellent quality, ethical standards, sound systems, and strong credibility.

PARTNER

Mrs. S. Sasikala

Mrs. S. Sasikala, a manufacturer and supplier of construction materials and a transport supplier, also actively functions as a Partner of Sekar Constructions. Her participation in the business has been highly supportive in securing uninterrupted supply of building materials and transportation.

PARTNER

Mr. S. Sudhangan

Mr. S. Sudhangan, a first-class graduate in Civil Engineering with up-to-date technical know-how, has been directly involved in all aspects of project management in the company. His active participation in Absolute Quality Control has further strengthened the company’s reputation for quality and speedy execution in recent times.

In addition to his role in project management, he plays a predominant role in the building materials industry in the Kalpakkam area. He serves as an active partner with M/s. Thirupathi Blue Metals, M/s. Seven Hills Mines, M/s. Pallava Mines, and M/s. Sree Balaji Blue Metals. He is also the owner of M/s. SS Bricks and Concrete manufacturing company. Thereby, Sekar Constructions enjoys the privilege of procuring all sorts of building materials effortlessly even during scarcities.
